Services & Methodology

Multi-Signal Detection

Spatial Analysis

Faces are isolated and analyzed by computer vision models looking for unnatural blending seams, texture inconsistencies, and digital artifacts.

Temporal Consistency

Tracks sequence-level details across frames including blinking frequency, lighting drift, and micro-movements between lip motion and speech.

File Provenance

Inspects metadata, encoder footprints, double-compression traits, and cryptographic signatures like C2PA manifest validity.

Known Limits

Full Transparency

Novel Generative Models

Unseen architecture types may lower detection confidence. Unrecognized patterns are treated as unresolved rather than clean.

Heavy Re-compression

Social platform re-uploads erase fine-grained camera noise. Degraded inputs are flagged accordingly.

Micro-Edits

Short edits inside long videos require timeline inspection; always check per-second bars rather than relying solely on global scores.
